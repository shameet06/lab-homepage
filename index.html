What are Index Funds?

An index fund is a type of mutual fund or exchange-traded fund (ETF) designed to match or track the components of a financial market index, such as the S&P 500 or the NASDAQ.

  • Standard index funds consist of a portfolio of all the different stocks in a certain index, and the weightages of each stock usually stays the same for long periods of time.
  • Active index funds are similar to standard index funds, but the amount of each stock in the index that the fund holds dynamically changes.
  • Niche Funds are a new type of index fund growing in popularity. These funds track narrow market segments, specific industries, or unique investment factors rather than broad benchmarks like the S&P 500.
